Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Travel Assistant is a personal learning project run by an individual developer, who is responsible for your data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: quangbach19112000@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google account identifier and email address, received when you sign in with Google. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not request your name, photo, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Your messages and the assistant's answers, stored per conversation so the assistant can follow up on earlier messages.
  • Preferences you mention that the assistant decides to remember (for example, "vegetarian" or "travelling with children"), so it can personalise later answers.
  • Technical records: request logs, timings, token counts and your feedback (thumbs up or down), used to run, debug and improve the service.

Why we use it

Only to provide the service you ask for: answering your questions, keeping your conversation history, and keeping the service reliable and within its usage limits. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles beyond the preferences described above.

Where it is processed

Your data is stored in a database hosted by Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden (EU). To answer a question, parts of it are sent to these service providers:

How long we keep it

We keep your conversations and remembered preferences until you ask us to delete them or the service shuts down, at which point all data is deleted. Technical logs are kept for a limited time for debugging.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing quangbach19112000@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
