Travel Assistant

Travel Assistant answers questions about cities, such as what to see, when to go, how to get around and what things cost. It answers from a curated set of travel guides, cites the guide it used, replies in the language you write in, and remembers your conversation and preferences you mention (for example, that you are vegetarian) so later answers fit you better.

How it works

You sign in with your Google account. Your question is matched against the travel guides, and an AI model writes the answer from the passages it finds. If the guides do not cover something, it says so instead of guessing.

Good to know

  • This is a personal learning project, not a commercial service.
  • The travel guides are illustrative. Prices, opening hours and rules may be out of date or wrong, so check official sources before you travel.
  • Usage may be limited to keep the service available for everyone.
